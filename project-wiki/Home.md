## Project Summary

**Problem:** The touch-screen monitor outside DSB 268 currently runs a timed slideshow of static informational slides. It does not showcase the department's senior design projects in an interactive, engaging way.

**Solution:** Build a kiosk-mode web application that allows prospective and current students to browse, search, and interact with past senior design projects — including descriptions, images, videos, and playable demos where appropriate.

**Target Users:**
- **Prospective students** — Exploring what CSE students build
- **Current students** — Finding inspiration for their own capstone projects
- **Faculty/Staff** — Showcasing department achievements during tours
- **Visitors/Alumni** — Reconnecting with past projects
