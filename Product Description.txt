Outside of DSB 268, there is a large touch-screen monitor that currently hosts a timed slide show of various CSE-related informational slides. This project involves finding a solution to using the display to host past senior design projects from the department (or other material, both visual-only or interactive, as appropriate). The end goal would be that this application can be launched on the big touch display so that a prospective student (or current student) could interact with it to see or try out previous projects that our students have engaged in.
